Neural networks are computing systems inspired by the structure of biological brains, composed of layers of interconnected nodes called neurons. Each connection carries a weight that is adjusted during training through backpropagation to minimize prediction error. Deep neural networks, which stack many hidden layers, are the foundation of deep learning and have driven breakthroughs in computer vision, speech recognition, and language modeling. Architectures like convolutional neural networks and transformers specialize in different types of data.
