Cloud computing delivers computing resources such as servers, storage, databases, and networking over the internet on a pay-as-you-go basis. Major providers like AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform offer services ranging from raw infrastructure (IaaS) to fully managed platforms (PaaS) and software (SaaS). Cloud computing enables organizations to scale resources dynamically, reduce upfront hardware costs, and deploy applications globally with high availability and built-in disaster recovery options.
