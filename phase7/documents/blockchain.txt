Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that records transactions across a network of computers in a way that is transparent, tamper-resistant, and decentralized. Each block contains a set of transactions and a cryptographic hash linking it to the previous block, forming an immutable chain. Blockchain underlies cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum, and its applications extend to smart contracts, supply chain tracking, and decentralized finance, offering trust without relying on a central authority.
